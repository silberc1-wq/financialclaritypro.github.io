Rules

These rules are used for every weekly report and printed on the schedule cards. Changes apply to the next file you check.

Breaks

No one works more than hours in a row. A punch-out counts as a break when it is at least minutes.

Coffee: anyone who punches in before must punch out at least once for minutes.

Dinner: anyone working after must punch out at least once for minutes.

Schedule

Count it as coming in early or leaving late when it's more than minutes off the schedule.

Skip break rules for

The schedule check (over hours, early, late) still covers everyone.
