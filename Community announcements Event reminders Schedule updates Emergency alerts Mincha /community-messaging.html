Community Messaging

FC PRO LLC provides community messaging systems that help organizations send informational SMS notifications to members and participants who have agreed to receive messages.

This community messaging program is used for informational and operational communications only. Messages may include community announcements, event reminders, schedule updates, minyan reminders, shiur reminders, urgent notices, and emergency alerts.

SMS Program Notice
Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance.

Types of Community Messages

  • Community announcements
  • Event reminders
  • Schedule changes and updates
  • Minyan reminders and timing updates
  • Shiur reminders and learning schedule updates
  • Urgent notices and emergency alerts

Who May Subscribe

Members, participants, parents, staff, volunteers, or other people affiliated with the participating community organization may subscribe to receive community-related SMS notifications.

Consent and Opt-In

Users may opt in by completing the signup form below or by texting JOIN to the designated community messaging number. By submitting the form or texting JOIN, the user agrees to receive informational SMS messages related to the community organization.
